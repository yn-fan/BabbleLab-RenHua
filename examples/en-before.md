# Trial notes

Here is the key takeaway: the trial is worth continuing, but the sample is still small.

On October 12, six colleagues tried the new editor. Four finished every task; two encountered an error while importing a file. We have only observed one trial, so we do not yet know whether the error will recur.

For the colleague who organizes our documents, the old editor took about 40 seconds per export and the new one about 25 seconds. Each figure comes from three measurements on the same computer. We did not test large files. We may invite the same group to try it for another week before deciding whether to expand the trial.

Chen said, “The buttons are easier to find, but I still don't understand where the history is.”

The account was not deleted; it lacks access to this folder. The administrator confirmed that the account still exists. Until the permissions are fixed, the two colleagues can keep using the old editor.
